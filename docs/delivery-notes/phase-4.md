# Phase 4 delivery note

The Phase Lead for phase 4 writes this note, during phase 4 — not afterwards, and not by whoever is free at the end.*
## What was delivered
- Every page shares one sidebar menu with sections (Placement, Reports, Evaluation, Help) and the same navy, yellow and white design. The ☰ button on the home page opens the menu, and Home on every page returns to the home page.
- A student can see each company's requirements, the system checks their completed credit units, and they can upload their documents. Files other than PDF, JPG or PNG are rejected with a clear message.
- A student can view their internship attendance log.
- A student can evaluate their training site from the home page.
- Pages show clear error messages instead of raw database errors.
- Storage buckets were created for uploaded files, and the sign-in screen was improved.

## What was delivered

- A student can view the approved training opportunities and the available internship cycles, select preferred and suitable internship sites, and apply for a training opportunity.
- A student can view their profile information, placement details and the company's requirements, and can optionally define their learning objectives.
- A student can log daily internship hours, track their total hours and view their attendance log. A day can be marked as sick leave with a medical certificate attached.
- A student can submit weekly progress reports and a final internship report, and view a summary of their progress.
- A student can report an internship issue and follow its status.
- A student can contact the internship coordinator or supervisor, and view the contact information for support from the side menu.
- A student can view the evaluation criteria, their grade and evaluation, and other students' reviews of internship sites. They can evaluate their training site, give feedback on the experience and request an internship certificate.
- A student receives university and application status notifications, is asked for notification permission, and can manage the privacy of their personal data.
- Each company has its own internship channel, with acceptance documents attached. The coordinator can review each student's internship status.
- Every page is reached from the main navigation menu.
